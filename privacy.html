Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This page explains how EvereSoft ("we") handles information on this website and in the TabTape browser extension.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. We don't collect personal information when you browse it. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and to provide support.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operations.

TabTape

Short version: your recordings and transcripts stay on your computer. We don't collect, upload or sell them.

What is stored, and where

  • Audio recordings you make, with their names, dates, durations and transcripts, are stored only in your browser (IndexedDB inside the extension). They are never sent to us or to any third party. Deleting a recording or uninstalling the extension removes them.
  • Settings (microphone preference, chosen speech model) are stored in the extension's local storage.

Microphone

The microphone is used only if you turn on "Also record my microphone", and only while you are recording. Microphone audio is mixed into the local recording and is not sent anywhere.

Transcription

Speech-to-text runs entirely on your device (Whisper model via transformers.js and ONNX Runtime). The first time you transcribe, the extension downloads the model files (about 40–200 MB) from huggingface.co. That download is a normal file request and contains no audio, text or personal data. Hugging Face may log standard request data (such as IP address) under its own privacy policy. After that, the model is cached and transcription works offline.

Payments (Pro only)

Purchases are processed by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) and Stripe. They handle your email address and payment details under their own privacy policies. The extension only asks ExtensionPay whether your license is active. We never see your full card details or your recordings.

Analytics

None. No tracking, no telemetry.

Your responsibility

Only record content you have the right to record, and follow the law where you live about recording conversations (some places require everyone's consent). TabTape does not get around copy protection (DRM).

Children

Our products are not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and requests

For questions or a privacy request (for example under the California Consumer Privacy Act), email everesofttech@gmail.com. We don't sell or share personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the update here with a new date.
