Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Cancel anytime

You can cancel a Pro subscription at any time:

  • open the extension's Settings → Pro → manage subscription (handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe), or
  • email everesofttech@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout.

Cancelling stops future renewals. Pro stays active until the end of the billing period you've already paid for. We don't charge cancellation fees.

Refunds

  • 14-day refund window: if you're not happy, email us within 14 days of a charge (the first purchase or a renewal) and we'll refund that charge in full.
  • After 14 days, payments are generally non-refundable. We'll still look at problems we caused, such as duplicate charges or a feature that doesn't work as described, case by case.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. They usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
  • When a charge is refunded, Pro access for that period may end.

How to request a refund

Email everesofttech@gmail.com with your checkout email address, the date of the charge and (optionally) the reason. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Free plan

The free plan costs nothing and needs no cancellation. Just uninstall the extension.
